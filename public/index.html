Usage reconciles the other two tabs into one picture. Hermes counts what hermes did, from hermes' own records. Engine counts everything the llama.cpp endpoint processed, including any other client on the network — hermes is one consumer among possibly several. Neither total is a subset of the other, and the part they share is counted once here: hermes' engine-hosted tokens are subtracted from the engine's total, so Hermes + Engine · other clients = Total.
